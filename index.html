ARROW ARENA

Prototype 0.6 · start with Classic, find stronger arrows, carry three · free-for-all against 6 bots · working title

How to play
W A S DMove
MouseAim
Left click (hold, let go)Draw the bow and shoot. A full draw flies faster and hits harder
Right click / F (hold)Shield. Blocks arrows from the front
SpaceDash. You cannot be hit mid-dash
Q / E, mouse wheel, 1 2 3Switch between the arrows you carry
ArrowsYou start with Classic and can carry 3 types. Find quivers on the ground, or pick one at every 3rd level. Tiers I, II and III hit harder; higher levels find better ones. With a full quiver, stand on a new one and press R (SWAP on a phone) to trade the arrow in your hand for it
TypesFire (burns) · Frost (slows, freezes) · Explosive (splash) · Piercing (goes through, breaks shields) · Poison (stacks) · Lightning (jumps)
XP dotsWalk over them to fill the XP bar. Each level lets you pick 1 of 3 upgrades
Power-upsRage (double damage), Haste, Shield bubble, Heal, and the rare gold Volley (a ring of arrows, then 10s of stronger shots)
EscPause, and change the difficulty. The game holds your mouse while you play

On a phone or iPad: left thumb moves, buttons on the right fight. Press SHOOT and drag to aim, let go to fire. Tap the arrow panel top right to switch. The game goes full screen when you press PLAY.

On iPhone, Safari cannot go full screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open the game from that icon.
